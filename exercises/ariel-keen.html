Ariel Keen Exercise

MGT 6400: Managerial Decision Making

Instructions

This tool serves two purposes:

• It allows you to "propose" multiple potential offers to Ariel Keen, and Ariel will let you know which offer she prefers (as indicated by how she ranks them).

• Once you have queried Ariel enough to determine a reasonably accurate scoring system, you can use this scoring system to score potential deals she may face.

Propose Some Offers to Ariel

The Propose Some Offers to Ariel tab allows you to enter up to six potential deals. Please attend to the error messages to make sure that you have entered the deals properly. Once the deals have been entered without errors, you will see the ranking of each of the deals from 1 (best) to 6 (worst). If two deals are tied, they will receive the same rank.

You do not have to enter 6 deals. You can enter any number of deals you like. Only the complete, error-free deals are ranked. Also note that you can enter deals without specifically assigning weights (to learn Ariel's preference ordering).

You should think of this part of the tool as simulating the process of asking yourself questions about possible deals. The aim of these questions is to help you understand your preferences, and eventually determine the missing values from the scoring system.

Negotiation Support

Once you have asked enough questions, you should feel comfortable providing the missing weights and scores for intermediate values (such as the score for Atlanta). Enter these in the Negotiation Support tab. Once all the scores are entered, you can see the point scores of all deals described on the Propose Some Offers tab.

Good luck!
